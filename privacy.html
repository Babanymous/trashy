2. In short

Trashy-0.1 looks at your pictures on your own device. I do not upload, store or see your pictures. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

3. Your pictures

When you choose a picture, your browser reads it and the AI running in your browser finds items in it and compares them with the trained model file. The picture, the boxes and the results are not sent to any server and are not stored. They are gone when you close or reload the page.

4. The model file

The page loads a trained model file from the same host. It contains numbers derived from the training pictures, not your pictures.

5. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, the host receives technical data such as your IP address, the date and time, the requested file and your browser type, and keeps it in server logs to deliver and secure the site. I do not use this data to identify you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in running a secure website). GitHub may process data in the USA; where required, the transfer relies on EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. Services loaded from other providers

To work, the page loads files from other providers. Each request sends your IP address and browser details to that provider:

  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): the TensorFlow.js and MobileNet code libraries.
  • Google model hosting (tfhub.dev and related domains): the MobileNet model files used to read pictures.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (showing the page and running the AI tool). Some of these providers are based in the USA or process data there. Where required, they rely on EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. The fonts (Bricolage Grotesque and Caveat) are stored on this website and are loaded from the same host, not from Google or any other provider. The legal pages use your system fonts.

I do not use cookies, local storage or similar tracking technologies.
