2. In short

Trashy looks at your pictures on your own device. I do not upload, store or see your pictures. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

3. Your pictures

When you choose a picture, your browser reads it and the AI running in your browser finds items in it and compares them with the trained model file. The picture, the boxes and the results are not sent to any server and are not stored. They are gone when you close or reload the page.

4. The model file

The page loads a trained model file from the same host. It contains numbers derived from the training pictures, not your pictures.

I do not use this data to identify you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in running a secure website). GitHub may process data in the USA; where required, the transfer relies on EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. No services from other providers

Everything the page needs is stored on this website and loaded from the same host: the code, the TensorFlow.js and MobileNet libraries, the MobileNet model files used to read pictures, and the fonts (Onest and JetBrains Mono). The page does not load anything from other providers, so no data is sent to them. Once the page has loaded, the AI runs entirely in your browser.

7. Cookies and local storage

I do not use cookies, local storage or similar tracking technologies.
